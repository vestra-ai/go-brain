Transit

Visa Free

No transit visa required (airside TWOV)

Apply
Pre-arrival registration required — complete before travel

Indian passport holders can transit through Thai airports (Suvarnabhumi, Don Mueang) without a visa when remaining in the international transit area. For landside transit, the standard 60-day visa-free admission applies.

Required documents
  • •Valid passport
  • •Confirmed onward flight ticket
  • •Visa for destination country (if required)
  • •Transit visa (if required for the layover country)

Apply
Pre-arrival registration required — complete before travel
Max stay

30 days

Processing

Not applicable (visa-free on arrival)

₹Fee

Free (visa-free entry)

30 days. Visa-free for a stay of up to 30 days (reduced to 30 days from 60 days effective 15 Sep 2026). From 1 May 2025, a digital arrival card must be filled, up to three days before arrival (TDAC).

Required documents
  • •Valid passport (at least 6 months validity beyond intended stay; 2 blank pages)
  • •2 recent passport-size photographs (white background, 35×45 mm)
  • •Completed visa application form (obtain from embassy or VFS/BLS centre)
  • •Confirmed return / onward flight bookings
  • •Hotel / accommodation reservations or invitation letter from host
  • •Bank statements for last 3–6 months (sufficient funds for duration of stay)
  • •Salary slips / income proof for last 3 months
  • •Employment letter with approved leave dates (or business registration if self-employed)
  • •Income Tax Returns for last 2 years
  • •Cover letter stating purpose of visit and itinerary
  • •Travel insurance (recommended)
